Let's start with location 1. In the image, the character is facing an island that's completely covered in ice - and checking the wiki map, there's only one such island in the Bronze Sea: **Frostmill Island**.

Looking back at `location_1B`, there are two islands I've circled in red; figuring out their names would tell us our position relative to Frostmill.
